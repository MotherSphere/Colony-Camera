[b]Description[/b]

Colony Camera is an independent third-person camera mod for Skyrim Special Edition. It adds smoother camera movement while retaining the game's native camera mechanics.

Position smoothing, adjustable offsets and shoulder switching let you customize how the camera follows your character. Three separate profiles cover exploration, movement with a weapon drawn, and aiming.

Version 0.1.2 is a functional alpha. It has been observed working in-game under Proton in a setup using Improved Camera, True Directional Movement and SkyParkour. This does not guarantee compatibility with every configuration or every animation provided by those mods.

Colony Camera is a separate project from SmoothCam. It does not require SmoothCam or reproduce its full feature set. This release does not include an MCM menu, a custom crosshair, projectile trajectory prediction or SmoothCam preset imports.

[b]Installation instructions[/b]

[list]
[*]Close Skyrim and back up your camera configuration.
[*]Install the requirements listed below for your exact game version.
[*]Disable SmoothCam and other full third-person camera replacements before enabling Colony Camera. Colony Camera remains inactive if SmoothCam.dll is loaded.
[*]Install the archive with your mod manager. The archive root contains SKSE, alongside licenses, notices and sources; keep these accompanying files.
[*]Check that the mod provides SKSE/Plugins/ColonyCamera.dll and SKSE/Plugins/ColonyCamera.ini. There is no ESP or ESL to enable.
[*]Launch Skyrim through SKSE, load a save or start a new game, and switch to third person. The camera hooks are installed after a game is loaded or started.
[*]Test movement, narrow passages, aiming and perspective changes before settling on your settings.
[/list]

Settings are stored in SKSE/Plugins/ColonyCamera.ini. Edit this file and press Ctrl+F10 to reload it in-game. Invalid configurations are rejected, leaving the previous settings intact.

For manual installation, copy the mod's SKSE folder into Skyrim's Data directory. To uninstall, close the game and disable the mod in your manager, or remove only ColonyCamera.dll and ColonyCamera.ini.

If you encounter a problem, check ColonyCamera.log in Documents/My Games/Skyrim Special Edition/SKSE. Under Proton, this location is inside the game's Windows prefix. Include this log in your report, along with your Skyrim and SKSE versions and the camera mods you use.

[b]Main features[/b]

[list]
[*]Progressive position smoothing based on elapsed time rather than a fixed number of frames.
[*]A configurable maximum lag distance to keep the camera from falling too far behind its target position.
[*]Separate profiles for exploration, combat with a weapon drawn, and aiming.
[*]Additional horizontal, depth and height offsets in camera space.
[*]Shoulder switching with Ctrl+F9.
[*]Enable or disable the effect with Ctrl+F8 to compare it with the native camera.
[*]Reload your configuration with Ctrl+F10 without restarting the game.
[*]Native collision handling applied after smoothing, before the final camera position is set.
[*]Smoothing resets after loading, cell changes, large teleports and extended update interruptions.
[*]First-person view, mounted cameras, dialogue and unsupported camera states remain under the game's control.
[/list]

Additional offsets default to zero, so the main out-of-the-box effect is smoothing. The aiming profile keeps the native position without additional smoothing to preserve crosshair alignment. Changing that profile may affect aiming accuracy.

In the INI, x, y and z control the offsets. half_life sets the time required to halve the remaining position error; max_lag limits the lag distance in game units. Keyboard shortcuts are configurable. Enable/disable and shoulder changes made with the shortcuts are not saved to the INI.

[b]Requirements[/b]

[list]
[*]Skyrim Special Edition, Steam edition, executable version 1.7.104.0. This DLL targets that version only and rejects other runtimes.
[*]SKSE64 2.3.1 for Skyrim 1.7.104.0.
[*]Address Library for SKSE Plugins, including the data for 1.7.104.0.
[*]Windows x64 or a compatible Proton setup. Testing under Proton does not guarantee support for every distribution or configuration.
[/list]

This alpha does not claim support for Skyrim VR, older runtimes or the GOG edition. SkyUI, an ESP and Papyrus scripts are not required. CommonLibSSE-NG is included at build time; you do not need to install it separately to play.

Improved Camera can remain enabled. Compatibility with other camera extensions should be checked in your setup, particularly during special animations and perspective changes. No FPS improvement or completely performance-free operation is promised.
